I've been drawing since childhood. I create OCs since being 5y.o.
It all started when mom brought us toys from boarding school. One of them was bear plushie, who later became one of my first chars, Myshko.
I've been dreaming about creating cartoons since early years, it really influenced my style - it's goofy and cartoony.
As time passed, I've been growing and creating more and more OCs, I've started liking game dev, thus I have started creating characters for my future games. The amount of my fictional universes had been growing and growing.

Among of things that influenced my style, I guess, were Disney cartoons and rubberhose style. I also was inspired by anime a bit.

I don't care much about realism, anatomy I consider only a bit. I start drawing my characters from poorly drawn lines and figures:

I draw like I want. I love to draw characters in jumpy dynamic poses. It kinda reminds me of Super Smash Bros. Ultimate renders style.
I like to draw different things. But often my chars have surreal designs.
